Privacy Policy — GemCascade

Effective date: August 6, 2026

GemCascade ("the app") is a casual match-3 puzzle game developed by Prudhvi Nelaturi. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. It does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

Where your game progress lives

Your game progress (level stars, coins, crystals, boosters, unlocked levels) is saved only on your own device, using local storage. It never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently.

Ads and analytics

The current version of the app contains no advertising and no analytics SDKs. If a future version adds ads or analytics, this policy will be updated first, and the app's Play Store data-safety section will reflect the change.

In-app purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Updates to this policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
